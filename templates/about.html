Platform overview

Academic learning, connected.

Smart DIT Learning Hub brings together module knowledge, guided support and clear learning signals.

{{ icons.icon('layers', size=18) }} Curriculum and content

Resources are organised by department, programme, academic level, semester and module. Lecturer-managed topics, outcomes, announcements and approved materials form the primary context for module learning.

{{ icons.icon('sparkles', size=18) }} AI learning support

The learning assistant uses the selected academic context and relevant resources first. Students can choose a simple, detailed, exam-focused or guided explanation style, review their conversation history and report unclear responses.

{{ icons.icon('bar-chart', size=18) }} Analytics with a purpose

Student insights focus on action: study consistency, topic activity, revision priorities and next steps. Lecturer dashboards show module-level engagement trends, learning time, topic activity and support requests for assigned modules.

{{ icons.icon('lock', size=18) }} Access and academic context

Student and lecturer portals are role-protected. Lecturer workspaces are limited to assigned academic contexts so module content and analytics remain relevant to the teaching responsibility.
